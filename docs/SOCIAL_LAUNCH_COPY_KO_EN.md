## Publishing rules

- Never say a fixed B3TR amount or payout date is guaranteed unless the product has explicitly adopted, funded, and published such a rule.
- Never say invitee mission completion equals an extra VeInvite payout to the invitee.
- State clearly that the inviter is the VeInvite referral-reward recipient for successful verified onboarding.
- Do not describe a projected, queued, prepared, submitted, or unfinalized amount as already paid.
- Do not claim that an onboarding completion guarantees immediate payout.
- Never ask users to send wallet seed phrases, private keys, or screenshots containing them.
- For support, direct users only to official VeInvite channels linked from the official link hub.
