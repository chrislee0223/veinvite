## Publishing rules

- Never say a fixed B3TR amount is guaranteed unless the product has explicitly adopted and funded such a rule.
- Never say invitee mission completion equals an extra VeInvite payout to the invitee.
- State clearly that funded VeInvite referral rewards, when enabled, are paid to the inviter for successful verified onboarding.
- Do not publish version B until mainnet reward distribution is explicitly enabled.
- Do not ask users to send wallet seed phrases, private keys, or screenshots containing them.
- For support, direct users only to official VeInvite channels linked from the official link hub.
